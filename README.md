# OrPath
OrPath

Let the Word Lead.

OrPath is a modern Bible and spiritual growth platform designed to help people explore Scripture, deepen their understanding of God's Word, build consistent prayer habits, and grow in faith.

Inspired by Psalm 119:105, OrPath aims to bring Bible reading, study, devotionals, prayer, and Christian community together in one accessible digital experience.

«“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.” — Psalm 119:105 (KJV)»

---

✨ Vision

To make Scripture more accessible and help people around the world develop a deeper, consistent relationship with God through technology.

🎯 Mission

To build an inclusive, secure, and user-friendly digital home for Bible reading, biblical learning, prayer, reflection, and Christian fellowship.

🚀 Planned Features

📖 Bible Reading

- Browse the books, chapters, and verses of the Bible.
- Search Scripture by keyword, passage, or reference.
- Bookmark favourite verses and passages.
- Highlight verses and add personal notes.
- Track reading progress.
- Compare Bible translations where licensing permits.
- Support accessible reading layouts and mobile-friendly navigation.

🧠 Bible Study Assistant

- Explore passage explanations and biblical context.
- Discover cross-references and related passages.
- Generate study questions and reflection prompts.
- Explore biblical themes, people, places, and events.
- Keep AI-generated explanations grounded in Scripture, with references and clear limitations.

🌅 Daily Spiritual Growth

- Daily Bible verse.
- Devotionals and reflection guides.
- Structured Bible reading plans.
- Prayer journal and personal reflections.
- Reading streaks and progress milestones.
- Optional reminders for reading and prayer.

🙏 Prayer and Community

- Maintain private prayer lists.
- Share verses and reflections.
- Participate in reading groups and community discussions.
- Submit prayer requests with privacy controls.
- Provide reporting, moderation, and safety tools for community spaces.

🎨 Personalisation and Accessibility

- Light and dark themes.
- Customisable reading settings.
- Responsive mobile, tablet, and desktop experience.
- Accessible typography and navigation.
- Offline reading for content that can legally be stored and distributed offline.

🔔 Notifications

- Optional daily verse reminders.
- Reading-plan notifications.
- Prayer reminders.
- User-controlled notification preferences.

🛡️ User Accounts and Security

- Secure authentication.
- Personal bookmarks, notes, and reading history.
- Privacy-conscious user profiles.
- Account settings and data controls.
- Role-based administration and moderation.

🛠️ Administration Dashboard

- Manage approved devotionals and reading plans.
- Manage announcements and community content.
- Review reports and moderation requests.
- Manage users and access permissions.
- View operational and engagement analytics.
- Maintain content attribution and licensing records.

💎 Sustainable Platform

- Carefully placed advertisements that do not obstruct Scripture or prayer.
- An optional premium, ad-free experience.
- Voluntary donations and supporter contributions.
- Transparent subscription and payment handling where enabled.

The app will prioritise a respectful reading experience. Advertisements will never be designed to interrupt prayer, obscure Bible passages, or exploit sensitive spiritual information.

📚 Bible Content and Licensing

OrPath will use Bible texts that are legally permitted for the intended use.

The King James Version (KJV) is a possible starting point, subject to appropriate verification of public-domain status in each target jurisdiction. Other translations may require permission, licensing agreements, or compliance with specific usage terms.

All third-party content, artwork, and resources must be attributed and used according to their respective licences.

---

🔐 Privacy and Responsible AI

OrPath aims to protect users' personal information and support responsible use of AI.

- Collect only necessary information.
- Protect private notes and prayer journals.
- Apply access controls and secure data handling.
- Avoid using private spiritual information for advertising targeting.
- Clearly distinguish biblical quotations from AI-generated commentary.
- Encourage users to check explanations against Scripture.
- Provide appropriate controls for account data and notifications.

---

🌍 Who Is OrPath For?

OrPath is being designed for:

- Individual Bible readers.
- Christians seeking consistent devotional habits.
- Bible study groups and churches.
- Students exploring Scripture.
- Families and reading communities.
- People who want a more organised prayer and reflection practice.

Installation and environment setup instructions will be added as the application structure is implemented.
